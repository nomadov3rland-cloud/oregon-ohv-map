Land colors — what they mean

National Forest — dispersed camping generally allowed
BLM land — dispersed camping generally allowed
State land — state forests yes (with rules), state parks no
National Park — campgrounds only
Other federal — refuges, military etc. — generally no dispersed camping
Tribal land — no, unless posted open
City / county land — check local rules
No color — private land, or closed to the public

The colors say whose land it is. On National Forest land the MVUM (the orange trails) is the legal word for motorized use. Camp away from water and developed sites, mind fire restrictions and stay limits.

About

Built from free public data: OpenStreetMap, U.S. Forest Service MVUM & trail inventory, Oregon Dept. of Forestry (trails & state forest boundaries), USGS PAD-US land ownership, U.S. Census Bureau (state & county boundaries), and AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM elevation). Trail and land data are navigation aids, not survey data — obey posted signs and closures; they beat this map. Works fully offline once the map packs are downloaded.
